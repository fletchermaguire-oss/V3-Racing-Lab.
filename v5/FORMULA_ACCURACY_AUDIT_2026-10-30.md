# V5 formula accuracy audit — 30 October 2026

**Purpose:** improve genuine future individual-horse win strike rate by better source evidence, better race-relative ranking and independent verification. **Status:** diagnostic only, not a formula change or new forecast. No coefficient tuning to 17/24 October winners. Keep V5-L4, V5-L5 and the independent 31 October rankings immutable for comparison.

## Concrete findings in current V5-L5

1. **Missing graded form is not always neutral.** In `v5/model.js`, `classQuality(starts)` returns `0` if no rated class is available. `calculate()` then applies `classWeight*(classQuality - oppositionAnchor)`. If known rivals have positive class ratings, missing graded evidence creates a negative score rather than a neutral unknown. This can systematically lower a horse with unparsed imported or unavailable class evidence. Candidate alternative: explicitly represent missing class as `null`, do not invent class quality, use zero *class contribution* for an unknown runner, and compute opponent anchors from observed peers only. **Do not implement as a production correction until paired comparison and sensitivity checks**, because this changes leader probabilities/ranks.
2. **Incomplete dated form remains material.** A syntax scan of the 30 October saved official-overlay-adjusted inventory yields 372 provisionally included runners, 36 excluded, covering 29 races. **23 / 372** have fewer than three recognisable dated performance lines (Randwick 5/134; Flemington 10/137; Ascot 8/101). This is a diagnostic line-count check, not the full `parseStarts()` acceptance audit. Example: Amazing Journey in Randwick R8 has one machine-readable overseas run whose source class `HCP` is unrecognised by the current ordinal class map. This is *insufficient evidence*, not proof of inferior ability.
3. **Actual effective weights aren't independently certified in the saved research.** `runnerFeatures()` compares `weight_kg` directly with field median. Only independently verified apprentice claims, actual riding weights and final declarations should update that input; no assumed claims.
4. **Quality-based priority filters are hypotheses.** `winFirstBoard()` requires floor >=17, gap >=4, etc.; these thresholds were not fitted out-of-sample. A strict screen can deliberately abstain from 26 races but has not been shown to improve *conditional hit rate* on forward unseen races. The high-conviction research list and screened model list must remain separate.
5. **Ordinal class and ability scaling are unvalidated.** The Group 1→maidens mapping, class weight (0.55 vs 0.28), temperature (0.78) and 15% uniform reserve have plausible modelling interpretations but no established effect sizes. Improving percentage calibration alone does not necessarily change winner ranking or hit rate.
6. **Historical comparison is not a strike-rate record.** 17/49 L5 retrospective top-one wins vs 16/49 L4 are from two reconstructed dates, with no proven independent pre-race freeze. The 24 October candidate Brier/log-loss worsened. Neither the difference nor replaying those winners justifies changing a live weight.

## Research priority, independent of the website

1. **Evidence completeness first.** For every contender and serious rival, reconcile full recent starts, dates, distances, official race class, race field and margins, verified weight/claim, barrier, gear change, pace/settling and going. Unavailable inputs remain marked unknown; never infer they are negative or favourable. Prioritize 23 thin-form runners and import/form-parse edge cases.
2. **Isolated mathematical-correctness experiment.** Build a separate candidate for neutral missing-class treatment, with regression tests for (a) truly absent graded data (b) all known graded peers (c) no known peer grades (d) shuffled runner order (e) equal field totals 100%. Compare race rankings and whole-field losses on same frozen inputs. Avoid horse-name rules.
3. **Transparent benchmark matrix.** Compare unchanged L4/L5, missing-class candidate and an optional data-enriched candidate on *identical* starter lists, with whole-field Brier, log loss, Top 1, Top 3, and number/percentage of races abstained. Report by meeting, race class, field size, and going when sample sizes allow. Retrospective checks are for bug detection, not claims of real improvements.
4. **Walk-forward pre-registration.** Freeze parameters, research cutoffs, fields, eligible candidate horses, timestamped rank and any reasons for abstention **before jump**. Keep a commit proof and official race-start data. Score independently verified results afterward; no reconstruction of a 'winning' shortlist. Compare on future meetings and don't promote a formula variant from isolated same-day anecdotes.
5. **Two distinct optimisation targets.** Measure (a) Top 1 for *all modelled races* and (b) a smaller high-confidence screen's wins / selections *and coverage*; also measure original research Top 5/10/15 separately. Never raise the displayed strike rate by simply dropping missed races after results.
6. **Calibration only on enough frozen history.** Existing `prospective-calibration.cjs` gate: ≥6 training dates/120 races and 2 later dates/40 holdout races, improved held-out Brier and log loss. Even temperature calibration does not guarantee improved Top 1 selection rate.

## Non-negotiable acceptance criterion

Call any accuracy improvement **demonstrated** only after genuine, date-ordered independent holdout or prospective results show better performance, with uncertainty/sample size disclosed and without material regression in data completeness or other groups. A small 1/49 retrospective gain is insufficient.

**No deploy, no original-site changes, no changes to Friday archives, independent Top 15, model coefficients, or published Results.**
