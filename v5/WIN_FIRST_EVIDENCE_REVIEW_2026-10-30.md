## Specific model limitations that affect hit rate

- **Underdeveloped imported/older form**: e.g. Perisher has only two accepted dated starts in V5; Rock Fest only one. These are **insufficient data**, not evidence of poor form. Resolve the entire credible career form before ranking.
- **Unfitted temperature/weights and ordinal class scores**: model % is a conditional hypothesis, not a genuine calibrated 40–60% win chance. Do not force numeric revisions to match market favourites.
- **Jockey claims / actual carried weight**: the static dossier `weight_kg` can differ from expected effective riding weight; claims and final declarations must be verified independently and any corrected model input applied consistently.
- **Ground and race shape**: Hot And High's untried Soft, Lady Shenandoah's settling position versus Surfin' Bird, Western Miss's gate 10, and Ascot R6 Good-v-Soft reversal are major independent inputs, not decorative notes.
- **Front-runner competition, class changes, uneven fields**: review serious rivals in the same race, not just a candidate's individual win rate.
- **Market disagreement is an audit alarm only**: prices do not enter model scores or independently prove an improved forecasting formula.
- **Automated strict three-horse screen is NOT a validated replacement** for the original Top 10 and should not be described as such.
