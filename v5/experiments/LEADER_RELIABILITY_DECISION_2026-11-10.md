# V5-L5 — reliability screening and shortlist ranking decision

Historical sample: 91 races across four dates. The original L5 first picks won **27/91 (29.7%)**. The fixed combination of a 20% L5 model probability, 5pp leader separation, 3 or more usable dated form starts, dossier completeness at least 70%, recognised class evidence, no input parsing problems and fewer than 15 active starters selected **10/91** races and won **7/10 (70%)**. No races qualified on 24 October or 3 November. The binomial Wilson 95% interval is approximately **39.7% to 89.2%**, and as all filters were investigated using known retrospective outcomes, this is **not** an unbiased forecast of any future strike rate.

The looser p≥20% and leader gap≥5pp selected **29/91** and won **13/29 (44.8%)**. Small fields (under 15) won **24/71 (33.8%)**, while larger fields returned **3/20 (15%)**. The sample is heterogeneous across dates. These flags primarily help us decide **whether to offer a selection** rather than who wins an individual difficult race.

Quality-aware ranking of unchanged L5 first picks, fixed penalties documented in the preregistration, retrieved one extra winner in the date-level Top 5 (**7/20→8/20**) and two extra winners in the Top 10 (**13/40→15/40**); Top 15 was identical (**19/60**). Not independently held-out; do not promote from a few changed races.

**Release decision:** keep current production L5 scores and ranking unchanged; preregister these rules as optional research-only screens for Saturday 14 November and freeze before race jumps. No automatic "lock" selections and no manufactured confidence probabilities. Compare forecast coverage, harm/gain and full-field score on later independently verified race results.
