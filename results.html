How this cumulative record is calculated

Earlier dates may use a different selection method; sources and rankings are preserved.

Top 5, Top 10 and Top 15 use each Saturday's recorded ranking and overlap; their counts must not be added together. Scratched runners never count as wins or losses. The older October 17/24 rankings were reconstructed from saved model forecasts, while October 31 uses the published original reviewed shortlist. This single combined outcome ledger is not a uniformly pre-race-certified, out-of-sample model accuracy statistic.

Underlying source archives remain available for traceability; the live results page uses one file: results-record.json.
